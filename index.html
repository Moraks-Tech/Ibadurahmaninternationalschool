ABOUT IBADU RAHMAN

Where Knowledge Meets Character and Faith

Founded in 2009, Ibadu Rahman International School is committed to providing qualitative and affordable education that prepares pupils and students for successful careers, responsible citizenship and meaningful leadership.

Our approach goes beyond the classroom. We seek to develop well-rounded individuals who are academically prepared, morally grounded, disciplined, responsible and conscious of their duties to Allah and humanity.

Through the Nigerian curriculum alongside comprehensive Islamic education, we create an environment where students can pursue academic excellence while developing sound character, faith and leadership qualities.

Vision: To build disciplined leaders — pupils and students prepared for the benefits of this world and the Hereafter — while making the school a reference point for others.
